When did I think it was a good idea to own one of mans greatest liabilities? I really can't put my finger on it!

I've grown up in proximity to boats but never really mixing, we spend our time windsurfing and kitesurfing. The idea of a yacht and exploring the world had always apealled to me. I think it was the prospect of picking a direction and just setting off, no white lines telling you where you can and can't go, no parking tickets (this is not true!), just an imagination of what's out there and the persistance to get there.

Well i find myself in the summer of 2024 owning a 1963 Kim Holman ketch, moored up in Falmouth.
