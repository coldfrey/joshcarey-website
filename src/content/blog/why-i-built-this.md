---
title: Why I built this site
description: A small, fast, owned corner of the internet.
date: 2026-05-20
---

I wanted a place that's mine — fast, clean, and simple enough that I'll actually
keep it updated.

The whole thing is static, built with [Astro](https://astro.build) and hosted on
Cloudflare Pages. Pages load instantly, there's nothing to maintain, and writing
a post is as easy as adding a markdown file.

If you're reading this, the setup works. 🎉
